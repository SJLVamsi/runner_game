# Budget Estimate: *Leela: Chronicles of Bharat*

This document provides the budget estimate for a 6-month development cycle and local setup for the cultural exhibition event. All figures are presented in United States Dollars (USD) and Indian Rupees (INR) at an exchange rate of $1 USD = ₹83 INR.

---

| Budget Category | USD (Est.) | INR (Est.) | Percentage |

| **Kiosk Hardware & Venue Router Setup** | $8,000 | ₹6,64,000 | 8% |

## 2. Category Details

### A. Development Team (6-Month Event Production)
* **Lead Three.js Developer:** $30,000 (₹24,90,000)
* **Lead Art & Asset Designer:** $25,000 (₹20,75,000)
* **Part-time Node.js Systems Developer:** $15,000 (₹12,45,000)
* *Note: Three.js and Javascript do not require Unity Pro or Unreal licensing fees.*

### B. Kiosk Hardware & Venue Network Setup
* **Arcade Style Joysticks, Keyboards & Monitored Kiosks (Rental):** $5,000 (₹4,15,000)
* **High-Performance Local Wi-Fi Router / Local Server Unit:** $3,000 (₹2,49,000)

### C. Hosting & Local Server Infrastructure
* **Static Web Hosting (AWS S3 + Cloudflare):** $500 (₹41,500)
* **Cloud Database & Live Leaderboard Server (Temporary event runrate):** $1,500 (₹1,24,500)

### D. Event Prints & QR Displays
* **Large PVC Banners with game QR codes:** $1,500 (₹1,24,500)
* **Exhibition Stall Decoration & Directional signage:** $1,500 (₹1,24,500)

### E. Audio & Localization (8 Indian Languages)
* **Regional Voice Actors for Story Scrolls:** $8,000 (₹6,64,000)
* **Custom Sitar & Flute soundtrack composition:** $4,000 (₹3,32,000)
