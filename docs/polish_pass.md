## 2. Animation Poses & State Synchronization

* **Running Loop:** Legs swing back and forth, and arms oscillate in opposite directions.
* **Jumping Pose:** Arms raise slightly, and legs tuck up to show vertical jump lift.
* **Sliding Pose:** The body squishes (scale Y = 0.55), arms tuck in, and legs extend forward to slide under arches.
* **Flight Pose:** Arms extend outward and legs align straight, mimicking a glider.
