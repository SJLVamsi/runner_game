# AAA Visual Improvement Plan: *Leela: Chronicles of Bharat*

This document outlines the visual mood, palette mapping, lighting strategies, and art direction for all historical locations in the game.

---

## 1. Location Art Direction Map

### Mathura (The Jail & Stone Fort)
* **Visual Mood:** Mystique, heavy stone fortress, divine anticipation.
* **Color Palette:** Burnt sienna, terracotta red, sandstone gold, dark indigo.
* **Architectural Style:** Ancient Indian prison stone masonry, heavy pillars, high iron arches.
* **Signature Landmarks:** Kansa's iron-bar cell, massive sandstone gateways, decorative cell arches.
* **Lighting Strategy:** Low-ambient indigo backdrop with high-contrast warm orange oil lamps (Diyas) casting sharp shadows on sandstone columns.
* **Environmental Storytelling:** Scattered broken chains on the track shoulders, wall inscriptions of Sanskrit prayers.

### Gokul (The Pastoral Pasture)
* **Visual Mood:** Serene, rustic, lush pasture, warm sunrise.
* **Color Palette:** Forest green, butter gold, pastel sky blue, birch tan.
* **Architectural Style:** Mud houses with straw thatch roofs, wooden pasture fences.
* **Signature Landmarks:** Nanda Baba's cottage, butter churning courtyards, riverside ghat stone steps.
* **Lighting Strategy:** Soft, diffuse bright gold sunlight with soft green hemispherical ambient lighting.
* **Environmental Storytelling:** Mud pots lined on wooden fences, grass patches, cows grazing in background dioramas.

## 2. Roadmap Locations Art Direction

### Vrindavan (The Forest of Basils)
* **Visual Mood:** Enchanting forest, divine play (Raas Leela), morning fog.
* **Color Palette:** Emerald green, peacock blue, blossom pink, golden rays.
* **Architectural Style:** Elegant forest canopies, sandstone step Ghats, ornate floral mandapas.
* **Signature Landmarks:** Yamuna River bank steps, Radha Ramana temple spires.
* **Lighting Strategy:** Volumetric light rays (God rays) filtering through dense green forest tree canopies.

### Dwarka (The Golden City)
* **Visual Mood:** Majestic sea city, golden glory, regal divinity.
* **Color Palette:** Pure gold, marine blue, pearlescent white, turquoise.
* **Architectural Style:** High coastal towers, golden gateways, grand pillared halls.
* **Signature Landmarks:** Sudarshana Temple arches, marine harbor bridges, golden fort walls.
* **Lighting Strategy:** High specular sea reflections, bright midday sun reflecting off pure gold structures.

## 3. Asset Requirements Checklist

1. **Character Skins:** Flute-playing pose, traditional peacock crown textures, dynamic sash/dhoti folds.
2. **Environment Props:**
   - Mathura: Iron chains, sandstone pillars, stone archways, lit clay lamps (Diyas).
   - Gokul: Hay bales, mud pots, wooden fence posts, low-poly forest trees.
   - Dwarka: Golden parapets, shell arches, pearl markers.
